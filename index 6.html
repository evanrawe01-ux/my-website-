New fan questions

College football, explained.

How is college football different from the NFL?

College teams represent universities. The sport has its own conferences, long-running rivalries, campus traditions, student sections and a national playoff.

Do I need to have attended an American university to support a team?

Not at all. Many fans choose a school because of its culture, family ties, location, traditions or a memorable first game.

Why are some games on so late in Europe?

American time zones mean an evening kickoff in the US often begins after midnight in Europe. Afternoon American games can be ideal European evening viewing.

Can I watch every game in my country?

Not necessarily. Rights, services and game availability differ by country and season. Check licensed providers for your location before subscribing.

What does a conference mean?

A conference is a grouping of universities that compete against each other regularly, often with a conference championship. Teams can also play opponents outside their conference.
